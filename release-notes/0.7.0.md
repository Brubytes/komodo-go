Komodo Go 0.7.0 is built around what needs your attention: a smarter dashboard, redesigned resource screens, and an app that uses less data and battery.

## New

- See what needs your attention on the dashboard, with one tap to the failing server, build, or deployment, or "All clear" when everything is healthy.
- Redesigned detail screens show a clear status header, put the most relevant action up front, and group settings into tabs and collapsible sections.
- Lists show problems first, and you can filter them by state and server.
- A new log viewer follows output live, opens fullscreen, and offers a raw text mode for selecting and copying any part of a log.
- Stop, Destroy, Cancel, and Run Action ask for confirmation, and you're warned before leaving a screen with unsaved changes.

## Improved

- Uses less data and battery: refreshes pause in the background, on hidden screens, and while you're offline, and slow down on cellular.
- The Resources overview groups your resources and shows at a glance which ones are failing.
- Notifications are easier to scan, with an Alerts and Updates switch, filter counts, and compact rows.
- Container cards show CPU, memory, network, and disk at a glance, with shorter image names.
- Procedures show their stages as a timeline.
- Animations are smoother and consistent, and respect your device's Reduce Motion setting.
- Signing in is easier: paste your API key and secret together, and enter your server URL in any common format.

Requires iOS 15 or later. Some operations depend on your Komodo version and permissions.

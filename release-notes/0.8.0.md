Komodo Go 0.8.0 makes full use of bigger screens, with layouts built for iPad and the foldable iPhone Duo.

## New

- Lists and details sit side by side on iPad and the unfolded iPhone Duo.
- A navigation rail replaces the bottom bar on wider screens, and moves into the side strip on iPhone Duo.
- Alerts have their own details screen, with a button to the affected resource.

## Improved

- The dashboard and detail screens use the extra width on bigger screens.
- Alerts and Updates share one layout, and alert rows have the same arrow to the affected resource as update rows.

Requires iOS 15 or later. Some operations depend on your Komodo version and permissions.
